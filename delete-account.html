Delete Your Dr. Scheduled Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Dr. Scheduled account and the associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account, send an email to:

drqrabbi@gmail.com

What to Include in Your Email

Please include the following so we can verify your identity and process your request:

  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Dr. Scheduled"
  • Your full name (as registered in the app)
  • Your registered phone number
  • Your 8-digit registration number (visible in your profile)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon verification, the following data associated with your account will be permanently deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, age, gender, phone number)
  • All registered family members under your account
  • Your appointment history (past and pending)
  • Any consultation notes and prescription images shared with you
  • Your push notification tokens
  • Login credentials

What Data May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons:

  • Anonymized appointment records — kept by treating doctors for medical record-keeping (as required by healthcare regulations). These records will not be linked to your name or contact information.
  • System audit logs — retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days of receiving your verified request. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

Partial Data Deletion

If you only want to delete specific data (such as appointment history) without closing your account, please mention this in your email and we will accommodate your request where possible.

Questions?

If you have questions about the account deletion process, contact us at drqrabbi@gmail.com.
