This Privacy Policy describes how the "Dr. Scheduled - Doctor" mobile application ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when used by registered medical professionals. The App is operated by Dr. Qaisur Rabbi ("the Developer").

By using the App, you ("the Doctor" or "the User") agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Who Can Use This App

The Dr. Scheduled - Doctor app is intended for use only by qualified medical professionals who have been registered by the App administrator. The App is not available for public download from the Google Play Store as a regular consumer app; it is distributed via an Internal Testing track. Accounts are provisioned by the administrator; users cannot self-register.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Professional information: Full name, medical designation, specialization, qualifications, years of experience, current workplace, areas of expertise.
  • Profile photo: A photo you choose to upload as your professional profile picture.
  • Authentication credentials: Username and password (stored as a hash where applicable).
  • Patient information you record: Clinical notes, diagnoses, and prescription photos you create while consulting registered patients.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device identifiers and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / FCM) used solely to deliver appointment notifications to your device.
  • Usage data related to in-app actions (e.g., scheduling, consultation completion).
  • Storage usage metrics for the photos you upload (sum of file sizes) to enforce your assigned storage quota.

3.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data (GPS, network, or otherwise)
  • Contacts from your device
  • Microphone or recorded audio
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers (we do not run ads)
  • Biometric data

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely to operate, secure, and improve the App. Specifically:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To display your scheduling calendar, appointments, and patient list
  • To deliver push notifications about upcoming appointments and bookings
  • To store and retrieve prescription photos and consultation records you create
  • To enforce your storage allotment as set by the administrator
  • To allow patients in the companion patient app to view your professional profile (name, designation, qualifications, photo)

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sale to third parties.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information is only shared with:

  • Patients you treat (limited): Patients using the companion app can see your name, designation, qualifications, current workplace, areas of expertise, and profile photo. Your phone number, email, password, and any other sensitive credentials are not shared with patients.
  • Administrator: The App administrator can view doctor accounts, storage usage, and reset passwords for support purposes. The administrator does not have direct access to your password (it is reset only).
  • Service providers (Google, Cloudflare), as listed in Section 5, only to the extent necessary to operate the App.
  • Legal authorities, only when required by valid legal process.

7. Patient Data Privacy

Although the App enables doctors to record clinical notes, diagnoses, and prescription photos about patients, we recognize that this is sensitive health-related data. Doctors using the App agree to:

  • Treat all patient information as confidential
  • Use the App only for legitimate medical consultations
  • Comply with all applicable medical confidentiality laws in their jurisdiction

Patient records you create are stored in our Firebase database and are accessible to:

  • The doctor who created them
  • The App administrator (for system operation purposes)
  • The patient themselves through the companion patient app (limited fields only — patients do not see prescription photos in this version)

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Authentication required for all data access
  • Cloudflare R2 access protected by a server-side authentication secret
  • Firestore security rules restrict data access by role
  • App store distribution signed with our private release key
  • No third-party advertising SDKs or trackers embedded in the App

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

  • Your account and professional profile are retained for as long as you use the App.
  • Consultation records and prescription photos are retained indefinitely so doctors can access historical records.
  • Notification records older than 7 days are automatically deleted.
  • If your account is deactivated, your professional profile information may be retained for legitimate operational, legal, or audit purposes.

To request deletion of your data, see Section 11.

10. Children's Privacy

The Dr. Scheduled - Doctor app is intended for use by adult medical professionals only. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

The companion patient app may record information about minor patients, but only when entered by a registered, consenting parent or guardian using the patient app. This Doctor app does not collect data directly from children.

11. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your account and associated data. (Note: clinical records you've created about patients may be retained per Section 9 for legitimate operational reasons.)
  • Withdraw consent: Stop using the App and contact us to remove your account.

To exercise any of these rights, email doctappointment@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

12. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications about new appointment bookings, cancellations, and reminders. You can disable notifications at any time through your Android device's settings (Settings → Apps → Dr. Scheduled - Doctor → Notifications).

13. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App includes the following third-party SDKs:

  • React Native (Meta Platforms, Inc.)
  • Firebase SDK (Google LLC) — for Firestore data, Cloud Messaging
  • Cloudflare R2 SDK (Cloudflare, Inc.) — for image storage
  • Notifee (Invertase Ltd.) — for native notification display
  • React Native Image Picker — for selecting photos from device gallery

These SDKs may collect minimal technical data (device model, OS version, app version) for crash diagnostics and SDK function. We do not enable advertising features in any SDK.
