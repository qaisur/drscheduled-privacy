Privacy Policy for Dr. Scheduled

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dr. Scheduled ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Dr. Scheduled, we collect the following information:

  • Personal information: Full name, age, gender, phone number
  • Account credentials: Registration number and password (encrypted)
  • Family member details: Names and ages of family members you add to your account
  • Appointment data: Doctor name, hospital, date, time, and serial number of your bookings
  • Health information: Consultation notes, diagnoses, and prescription images shared by your doctor (visible only to you and the treating doctor)
  • Device information: Push notification token (FCM token) used solely to deliver appointment notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your patient account
  • To book and manage doctor appointments
  • To send you push notifications about appointment confirmations, cancellations, and reminders
  • To allow your treating doctor to record consultation notes and prescriptions
  • To verify your identity through OTP (one-time password)

3. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Google Firebase Firestore — for appointment, account, and consultation data
  • Cloudflare R2 — for storing prescription and consultation images

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only accessible to:

  • You (the registered patient)
  • The doctor you have booked an appointment with (limited to appointment and consultation details)
  • The system administrator (for account support and password reset purposes)

5. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send you notifications about your appointments. You can disable notifications anytime through your device settings.

6. Data Retention

Your account and appointment history are retained for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for use by adults (18+) booking appointments for themselves or their family members (including minors). Minors should not use the app independently.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted database connections, Firebase security rules, and password hashing. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
