Privacy Policy

Dr. Scheduled - Doctor-Patient Appointment Scheduler

Effective Date: May 1, 2026 | Last Updated: May 1, 2026

Dr. Scheduled ("we", "our", "us") operates the Dr. Scheduled mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register and use the App:

  • Personal Information: Full name, date of birth, age, sex, phone number
  • Health Information: Current health problems/concerns described during registration
  • Account Credentials: Registration number, password (stored securely)
  • Appointment Data: Booking details, doctor preferences, appointment history
  • Medical Records: Consultation notes, prescriptions, and medical history as recorded by your doctor
  • Device Information: Device token for push notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your patient account
  • Booking and managing doctor appointments
  • Sending appointment confirmations, reminders, and notifications
  • Enabling doctors to access your medical history for consultations
  • Phone number verification via OTP (One-Time Password)
  • Improving the App's functionality and user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, a cloud database service provided by Google. We implement the following security measures:

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase security rules restrict unauthorized data access
  • Medical images and prescriptions are stored in secure cloud storage (Cloudflare R2)
  • Push notification tokens are stored separately and used only for notification delivery

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • With Your Doctor: Your registered information, medical history, and appointment details are shared with the doctor(s) you book appointments with
  • Hospital Receptionists: Authorized receptionists may access your name and phone number for walk-in appointment booking
  • Service Providers: We use Google Firebase for data storage and authentication, and Cloudflare for image storage. These providers have their own privacy policies
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Phone Number Verification

We use Firebase Phone Authentication to verify your phone number via SMS OTP during registration and appointment booking. This service is provided by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy. Your phone number is used solely for verification purposes and is not shared with any other third parties.

6. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications about appointment bookings, cancellations, and reminders. You can disable push notifications through your device settings at any time.

7. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Appointment records are retained for medical record-keeping purposes
  • Read notifications are automatically deleted after 24 hours
  • All notifications are automatically deleted after 7 days
  • You may request deletion of your account by contacting us

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data through the App
  • Update your information through your patient profile
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications through device settings

9. Children's Privacy

The App allows registration of minors (children) by their parents or guardians using the family registration feature. A parent or guardian's phone number is used for all family members. We do not knowingly collect information from children without parental involvement.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
